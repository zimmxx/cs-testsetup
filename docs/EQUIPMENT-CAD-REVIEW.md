## Provenance and files

- Vendor product asset metadata and drawings are stored beside the Thorlabs CAD. The Keithley STEP came from the official [6487 CAD archive](https://www.tek.com/en/documents/cad-files/model-6487-3d-cad-models).
- FPC562 and MAX313D keep the original files supplied by the user. Newly retrieved vendor files are retained separately; a revised vendor file does not silently replace the user-selected geometry.
- Generated CAD has a provenance.json and a parts sidecar. It uses closed planar faceted BREP solids; it is not a native SolidWorks parametric feature tree.
- Browser conversions have metadata with source file, axis convention, tessellation, extents and triangle count. Equipment records contain STEP SHA-256 hashes.
- Supplier CAD/documents remain supplier-owned. Review redistribution terms before public GitHub publication.

The 81940A reference is based on the [Keysight 819xxA datasheet](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/assets/7018-01142/data-sheets/5988-8518.pdf), which supplies its 75 H × 32 W × 335 D mm body envelope. The cover photo is of a related 81960A module; front details and vents are approximate family geometry. The [8163B datasheet](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/assets/7018-01037/data-sheets/5988-3924.pdf) supports the mainframe body envelope. The [E364xA guide](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/assets/9018-01165/user-manuals/9018-01165.pdf) supports the E3640A body envelope. Generated details and clearances are not measured.
