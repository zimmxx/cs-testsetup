## Inferred / incomplete information

Fibre-array C-band and planned edge-coupling C/O-band catalog allocations are provisional and explicitly flagged. Room location for visible and MIR work is unconfirmed. Source, detector, coupling hardware and polarisation models for band-specific and wafer layouts are reference families until verified locally. Manufacturer links to Thorlabs lead to the vendor homepage when an exact installed part is not recorded.

Guides are authored draft outlines. Planner assumptions are illustrative. No acquisition timings or setup availability are inferred from an image.
